# Credits

## Nergal, the mascot

The demon in chaos-bringer's mascot is based on the horned scythe-wielding
demon from **"6 More RPG Enemies"** by **Stephen "Redshrike" Challener**, with
Blarumyrran and LordNeo, hosted by OpenGameArt.org:

- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/6-more-rpg-enemies
- License: [CC-BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) /
  [OGA-BY 3.0](https://opengameart.org/content/oga-by-30-faq)

**Changes made for chaos-bringer:** the demon's skin was recolored from red
to plague green, glowing eyes were added, the scythe was split onto its own
layer and rocked around the demon's hand so it stirs the cauldron, and the
demon was rim-lit by the brew with its legs shadowed behind the pot. The
cauldron, brew, fumes, spill animation and backdrop were drawn from scratch
for this project.

The unmodified source sheet lives at `tools/mascot/more_rpg_enemies.png`;
`tools/mascot/build_mascot.py` rebuilds every derived asset from it
(`docs/nergal.gif`, and `src/chaos_agents/mascot_data.py` via
`tools/mascot/export_terminal.py`).
